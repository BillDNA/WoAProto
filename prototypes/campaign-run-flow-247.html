What to react to (the open questions this napkin exists to settle)

  1. Which map layout? The Front (attrition-native, but linear), The Climb (legible, generic), or The Theater (hex, echoes the battle board — richest but heaviest to build). Pick one to carry into the build ticket, or a hybrid.
  2. Is the front door a run? New Campaign drops you straight onto the map — no flat hub. Right feel, or do you want a between-runs home first?
  3. Node → Battalion builder every battle, or only at the start of a run? Right now every node re-opens the builder. Tedious, or the point?
  4. How much branching? This graph forks twice per act. More agency, or more paralysis?
  5. What does "attrition" cost between nodes? Strength carries over and only Muster heals — does the map need to show that pressure, not just the ledger?
